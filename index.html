The Skardu Scale-Up Fellowship

Design for Scale

June 7–14, 2026 | Khoj Resorts, Skardu | Co-hosted by Taleemabad & Mulago Foundation

Program Overview

The Skardu Scale-Up Fellowship brings together 11 promising social enterprises from Pakistan for a 7-day intensive retreat designed to transform organizations from project-driven survival mode to scale-ready impact machines.

The Challenge: Pakistan has dozens of promising social enterprises, but few achieve true national scale. Most remain trapped in project-driven survival mode, securing incremental grants and reaching thousands when their models possess the potential to reach millions.

The Solution: A curated, rigorous retreat where founders step away from daily operations to answer the defining strategic questions of scale: Who is your ultimate doer (the entity that implements at scale), and who is your ultimate payer (who funds it)?

Vision & Impact

We're transforming organizations from project-driven survival mode to scale-ready impact machines using the Mulago Foundation's proven Scale Screen framework.

The Four Enoughs

  • Good Enough: Does the solution actually work, with credible evidence?
  • Big Enough: Is the problem large enough to matter at scale?
  • Simple Enough: Can others replicate it without the founder?
  • Cheap Enough: Can the ultimate payer afford to reach everyone who needs it?

Your role: Help founders architect for national and systems-level impact.

The Fellows

11 leaders across mental health, disability, health tech, education, livelihoods, legal, and agriculture.
